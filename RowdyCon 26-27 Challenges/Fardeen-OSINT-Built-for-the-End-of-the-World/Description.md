# Built for the End of the World

## Description


Look, I work in a nuclear facility and my PRIMARY worry is that my laptop won't survive if a disaster occurs. While I was watching Evangelion, I saw that they were using a particular laptop that looked quite sturdy. I think this is the laptop that would ease all my worries. Can you find out what laptop it is so that I can go out and buy it? I just need the model number.

## Files

![Film frame showing a laptop in use](scene.jpg)

Submit `RCC{model}`. The model family is enough.

## Walkthrough

1. Reverse-search the frame or search for `Evangelion Paris laptop`. [ITmedia's article](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/article/2103/10/1210310096/3) places the computer in the opening Paris operation of **Evangelion: 3.0+1.0** and identifies it as a likely **Panasonic Toughbook CF-19**.

   ![The saved ITmedia article identifies the WILLE crew's computer as a likely TOUGHBOOK CF-19.](screenshots/01-article-identification.png)

2. Compare the central screen hinge, protected corners and speaker grille with the article's [real product photograph](evidence/real-laptop.jpg). The [Panasonic brochure](https://www.marinea.fi/downloads/Toughbook%20CF-19.pdf) shows the same convertible family and prints **TOUGHBOOK CF-19** on its cover.

   ![Original Panasonic brochure cover showing the convertible and CF-19 designation.](evidence/brochure-page.png)

3. Open [Panasonic's Evangelion history](https://ec-plus.panasonic.jp/store/page/product/evangelion/) and find **HISTORY 04: WILLE支給ノートPC**. Its model is **CF-19 タフブック** and its film is **シン・エヴァンゲリオン劇場版**. The preceding CF-F8 entry belongs to a different film; the requested model is **CF-19**.
