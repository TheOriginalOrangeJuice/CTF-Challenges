# A Souvenir with a Moveset

## Description

My friend got this invitation to a launch party of some Digimon game. He apparently got a free monster that had a curved horn and was holding a "Ganlon Berry"? It's also somewhat rare because it came with a move that it normally couldn't learn from the original game? I really want to know what move that was; can you tell me the English name of that special move?

## Files

[invitation.jpg](../player/invitation.jpg) — one original promotional leaflet. Submit `RCC{move_name}`.


## Hint(s)

1. The invitation identifies a real event. The creature description identifies a species; the berry distinguishes the gift.
2. This was a Pokémon downloaded at the store's station, not a reward earned inside the Box software.
3. The promotion included two Absol variants. Preserved event records distinguish the Ganlon-bearing one from the Salac-bearing one.

## Walkthrough

1. Read the invitation: **Pokémon Center New York**, **Pokémon Box**, **July 10, 2004**. The white Pokémon with a curved horn is **Absol**.

2. Search for the Pokémon Box launch promotion. The [leaflet's reverse](https://pokemonhistorian.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Box-leaflet-back.jpg) advertises a download at the **Gotta Catch 'Em All station**. The [contemporary Pokémon.com announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20040805124345/http://pokemon.com/events/index.asp?articleID=534) also mentions the special download.

   ![The original leaflet invites visitors to download a Pokémon at the station.](screenshots/01-download-station.png)

3. Find the Pokémon Box section of [PokéHistorian's Generation 3 campaign research](https://pokemonhistorian.com/pcny-project/pcny-campaigns-gen3/). Its **Ganlon Berry** Absol has **Wish**; the **Salac Berry** version has Spite. The berry in the description selects **Wish**.

   ![The campaign entry distinguishes the Ganlon/Wish and Salac/Spite variants.](screenshots/02-berry-move-variants.png)

4. Cross-check the [preservation register](https://pokemonhistorian.com/pcny-project/pcny-preservation-records/), under **Inkydog26 Collection → Pokémon Box**. It lists **PCNYd 00200 Wish Absol** alongside other Wish and Spite examples. These collection records support the move; the official invitation does not name it.

   ![Preserved Wish Absol entries, including PCNYd 00200, followed by separate Spite examples.](screenshots/03-preserved-collection.png)
