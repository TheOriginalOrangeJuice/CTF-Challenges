## Original image prompt (built-in tool)

The final layout was also compared directly with the [period Vegas Pro12 overview screenshot](https://www.proaudio.de/images/stories/tests/sony_vegaspro12/overview.jpg), frozen privately as `source/vegas12-overview-reference.jpg`. It informed the neutral-gray workspace, media/Trimmer/preview composition, stereo Master meters and larger timeline. Event controls live in a working Edit Details dock tab rather than occupying the default Trimmer view. Pan/Crop properties are on the left of the source canvas. The custom raw inspector and simplified editing subset remain explicit differences; exact native-software equivalence is not claimed.

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original endcard artwork for an affectionate 2013 MLG meme montage in a Windows 7 Sony Vegas style editor. Create one polished wide 16:9 illustrated endcard, 1536x864 if possible. Full frame energetic collage: emerald and acid-green neon lightning, cyan laser beams, chrome metallic wings, golden tortilla-chip confetti, an illustrated orange cartoon air horn, black pixel sunglasses, and a brilliantly rendered emerald pyramid with a radiant eye at its apex. Specific early-2010s meme montage maximalism, saturated high contrast, airbrush/photo-collage texture and punchy glossy reflections. Upper left an oversized speaker cone with green sound rays, lower right a tilted soda can with no branding. Main central composition: a broad continuous dark navy-black horizontal banner centered across full width, extending from x=100 to x=1436, y=320 to y=544, perfectly blank for a later title. Bright glowing double cyan/green trim around this banner, with a diagonal glowing green slash and elaborate chrome ornament crossing above and below the center. The illustration will be split into six adjoining rectangular pieces, so continuous bright lines and recognizable shapes must cross every vertical third and the horizontal center and allow visual jigsaw alignment. No text, no numbers, no letters, no watermark. No interface or desktop. Dense detailed artwork, not a flat vector placeholder. Entire canvas opaque.

## Reproduction

Use the pinned organizer requirements and the frozen source assets. `generate.py` writes PNG carriers directly from unassociated RGBA rows, derives the soundtrack from frozen decoded excerpt PCM, creates project data, thumbnails, private solution/master, stable ZIP ordering/timestamps and digests. The decoded audio, encoded gameplay derivative and rasterized logo are frozen after their first conversion and reused; bit-for-bit repeated generation is checked against those same frozen derivatives. Recreating them with different Chromium/ffmpeg versions is outside that repeatability claim. `generate.py --audio-only` rebuilds the soundtrack, archive and hashes without rewriting PNG evidence. The application has no package-manager dependencies.
