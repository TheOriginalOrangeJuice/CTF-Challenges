Welcome to your igloo!

Click the floor to waddle over. You can also focus the room and use the arrow keys.

Click a puffle to open its care card. Pip's translator lets you chat using the bar at the bottom. The tab at the top opens your full conversation.

Use the wrench to decorate. Choose an item, click to place it, or drag it around. Select Save to keep your changes, or Cancel to put everything back.

The toolbar has emotes, penguin actions, snowballs, your player card, and settings. Your postcards are in the envelope at the top.
