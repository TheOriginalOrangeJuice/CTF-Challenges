The Impossible Security+ Quiz
Misc / Logic · Hard–Very Hard
Description   Your Security+ exam has been replaced by SY0-NOPE. The
              proctor is a goose. The biscuits are classified. The
              accreditation is questionable.
              Pass twenty questions and convince the exam board that you
              are provisionally employable. You have three lives. The goose
              has tenure.
Files         The player opens the hosted quiz; there is no player
              download. Deploy challenge/ with Docker Compose. Keep this
              document and organizer/ private.
              From this challenge's challenge directory:
              docker compose --env-file ../organizer/event.env up --build -d

              Open http://localhost:18115. The private event.env supplies the
              fixed flag. For an event hostname, set PUBLIC_ORIGIN to that
              exact origin; set COOKIE_SECURE=true behind HTTPS. SQLite
              persists sessions in the quiz-data volume. Stop this
              deployment with docker compose down; retaining the volume
              preserves player progress and certificates. Each browser
              cookie identifies a separate session. Deploy only the
              application image, never the whole challenge folder as a web
              root.
              Original effects, music, and reference artwork are frozen
              locally. Their provenance and unresolved redistribution
              permissions are recorded in asset-manifest.json. This
              package does not establish permission to redistribute third-
              party music.
Hint(s)       None
Walkthrough   The exam rewards checking the evidence behind a label.
              Early questions introduce physical boundaries, exact
              permissions, a photographed strip, trust rules, and clock
              corrections. Later questions combine those observations into
              one final audit. Every necessary technical rule appears in the
              quiz.
              Click START. Inspect and change drafts freely; ANSWER
              submits them. A wrong answer normally costs one of three
              lives. Question 16 warns that detonating its circuit ends the
              entire attempt. Save the skips earned at questions 5, 10, and
              15. Spending one scouts ahead but requires a fresh attempt
              before certification. NOTES retains evidence and personal
notes across deaths; DOWNLOAD NOTES saves a text copy.
Reload and RESUME recover the current attempt.




1. AUTHORIZE THE ANSWER. Only words outside a box
may be authorized. The four pink boxes fail that rule. Select
THE ANSWER within the question heading, then press
ANSWER.
2. Patch the critical vulnerability. Biscuits fall through the
tear in the goose's trousers. Drag CLOTH PATCH onto the
goose, or select the patch and activate Torn trouser seam
with the keyboard. The patch stops the leak. Submit the
patched state.
3. Your password must be eight ticks long. Measure the
drawing. Stretch its right handle until PASSWORD spans at
least eight ruler intervals. The Stretch handle slider also
works with arrow keys. Submit a width of eight or more.
4. Two factors, please. The categories distinguish
remembering something, carrying something, and a live
physical characteristic. PIN and password are both KNOW.
Choose any two cards from different categories, such as
PASSWORD and SECURITY KEY, then submit. A security
key and live wingprint also qualify.
5. Put ONLY the ducks inside the firewall. Both left
pictures are ducks despite their captions. Move and resize
the pink boundary to contain both and exclude the brick and
laptop on the right. Arrow keys move the focused boundary;
Shift plus arrows resize it. Submit and keep the first skip.




6. Air-gap the vault. V has two routes to the public
network: A–V and B–V. Cut both using the cables or their
buttons. Keep I–A, I–B, A–B, and V–L connected. This isolates
V and its printer L while preserving the public soup network.
7. The intern requested EVERYTHING. The job needs only
READ. Toggle PRINT, APPROVE, and SHRED off, leaving READ
enabled. A useful-sounding permission is still excessive when
the job does not require it.
8. Find the false positive. Hover or focus each bag to
inspect its contents. A false positive is an alarm on something
the policy permits. Bag 3 contains a rubber duck and raises
an alarm. Select it, confirm its selected state, and submit.
The knife without an alarm is a false negative.
9. Back up the original. Not the mistake. The intake
photograph shows A B C D, all faces up. Use MOVE PAPER
and, if helpful, DIRECTION to bring that actual strip into the
capture window. Press COPY. The photograph remains in the
notebook. Conveyor direction changes movement, not the
order of printed symbols.
10. Chain of trust. Read STAMP OFFICE, then open
APPLICANTS. At 12:01, Carrot's grant to Goose has not
expired, and Goose may delegate OPERATE to Moth. Frog has
only VIEW; Eel has no path to the pinned root. Select Goose
and Moth. TEST SELECTED STAMPS · FREE demonstrates
the policy without costing a life. Submit and retain the
second skip.




11. No tailgating. Use Add action to draft: Open outer →
Goose enters vestibule → Close outer → Open inner → Goose
enters room → Close inner. REHEARSE · FREE shows the
simulated result. Only the authorized goose enters, the
follower stays outside, and both doors end closed. Submit.
Equivalent safe sequences are accepted.
12. The clocks have unionized. The same noon bell reads
BOARD 12:04 and PRINTER 11:57. Subtract four minutes from
BOARD and add three to PRINTER. The panels occurred at
BACKUP 12:00, ISSUE 12:01, APPROVE 12:02, and EXPIRE
12:03. Select those panels in that order. Clicking a selected
step removes it; Clear plan starts the draft again.
13. Keep the DING. Lose the DOOM. A contains noise plus
the desired signal. B contains the same noise delayed 200
ms. Set B position to −200 ms, then choose B UPSIDE
DOWN. Aligned negative noise cancels the noise in A; A+B
matches the pink reference. MIX plays the actual computed
samples. Submit. The waveform and 100 ms grid provide
equivalent evidence while muted.
14. The checksum is an idiot. Every choice totals ten
under the stated addition rule. That says nothing about
order. Open NOTES and compare question 9's original
photograph. Select ABCD and submit.
15. Audit the auditor. Stamp A and B TRUE, C and D FALSE.
A agrees that B is true; B agrees that C is false. A and D
differ, making C false. Exactly two of A, B, and C are true,
making D false. All four stamps agree with their claims.
Submit and retain the third skip.
16. Four decisions until kaboom. Read the circuit
interlocks before committing. Removing power while the
relay is armed detonates; draining a connected capacitor
recharges it; battery removal does not remove stored charge.
Draft ISOLATE RELAY → REMOVE BATTERY → DRAIN →
CUT WIRE. Then press EXECUTE. There is no timer; editing
the four-step plan is free.
17. Put NOT where it belongs. The cards require denying
only the round-and-striped object. Choose NOT (ROUND
AND STRIPED). The negation covers the whole conjunction,
so anything missing either property is allowed. Confirm that
all four computed examples match their wanted outcomes,
then submit.
18. Backwards is not the same as undone. From C− B+
D+ A+, F · FLIP FIRST restores C's face; S · SWAP FIRST
TWO makes B+ C+ D+ A+; R← · LAST TO FRONT restores
A+ B+ C+ D+. Submit. Undo last experiment and Reset
machine allow free exploration. Any sequence restoring
every identity and face is valid.
19. One change label was forged. The first photograph
moves A to the end, matching R. The second swaps B and C,
matching S. The third preserves the order and flips C's face,
so its label must be F. Set Change label 3 to F · FLIP
FIRST, keeping R and S for the first two. Submit.
20. CERTIFY THIS. Work through three views of one board.
Drafts remain when switching views. The notebook retains
the stamp policy, clock calibration, and machine rules.
In 1. AUDIT, correct every signing time and check the
complete authority chain. Ticket 1 is Goose at 12:00; ticket 2
is Moth at 12:01; ticket 3 is Goose at 12:02. These carry valid
OPERATE authority. Put skips on 4, 5, and 6: Moth's
ancestor has expired by ticket 4's corrected 12:04; Frog has
only VIEW on ticket 5; Eel has no trusted root on ticket 6. Tap
a ticket again to remove its skip.




In 2. MACHINE, choose valid tickets 1 → 2 → 3 in corrected
chronological order. Their F, R, S transformations produce:
A+ B+ C+ D+
A− B+ C+ D+   F: flip first
B+ C+ D+ A−   R: move first to end
C+ B+ D+ A−   S: swap first two

In 3. ISSUE, compare only exposed faces. Slot 1, C+,
includes APPROVE; slot 2, B+, includes APPROVE and lacks
READ; slot 3, D+, lacks READ. Slot 4, A−, grants exactly
READ and PRINT. Select it and press CERTIFY. Success
consumes all three skips atomically and reveals the flag.
COPY FLAG copies it; the menu's Your certificate recovers
it later.
