# K-ON! — Before Departure

## Description

The night before March 16th, 2011, I dreamed about me and the gang going to some store and shopping for some souvenirs. The next day, I watched the gang ACTUALLY go to the store; I unfortunately could not be there. Now that I have adult money, I want to go to this store they went to and relive my dream. Can you find me the store name that they went to? I don't want the new store name ('cause who cares); I want the name of the retailer when the gang went there. (Which retailer used to run the shop associated with this scene?)

## Hint(s)

1. The girls are preparing to travel. Identifying the episode will help place these scenes.
2. The passport-office interior corresponds to the basement of Tokyo Metropolitan Government's Citizens' Plaza.
3. A Japanese visitor in 2017 recorded what a photo-service employee remembered about the previous shop.

## Walkthrough

1. Identify the travel-preparation episode, **“Plan!” / 計画！**, listed as season 2, episode 27. The [episode listing](https://tv.apple.com/jp/episode/%E8%A8%88%E7%94%BB/umc.cmc.28kfgpaexkkemseb8v8f3y8mu) describes the passport preparations that connect the two scenes.

2. Search `けいおん 計画 旅券 聖地` for pilgrimage reports about the passport office. The [September 17, 2017 visitor report](https://gfi402.hatenablog.jp/entry/2017/09/17/141236) places it on **Citizens' Plaza B1 at the Tokyo Metropolitan Government complex**. Match the glass corridor and yellow sign to image 2.

   ![The visitor's corridor photograph, matching the glass frontage and yellow sign in image 2](evidence/corridor-2017.jpg)

3. Read the paragraph beneath the Ritsu frame. The visitor reports that the later ID-photo area previously housed an **Isetan travel-goods shop**. The following shopping frame and paragraph connect that former shop to the anime scene. The retailer is **伊勢丹 — Isetan**, not the photo service.

   ![The visitor's paragraph naming Isetan as the former travel-goods retailer](screenshots/02-former-tenant.png)

4. Corroborate the historical retailer on PDF page 2 of the [official notice](https://www.vill.miyake.tokyo.jp/bousai/kiroku/files/w1310b.pdf). It names **伊勢丹都庁総合売店** and places it beside the passport center on Citizens' Plaza B1. Submit `RCC{isetan}`.

   ![Original notice naming the Isetan shop and its basement location beside the passport center](screenshots/03-historical-shop.png)
