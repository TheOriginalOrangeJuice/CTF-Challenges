# My First Gaming Clan

**Category:** Misc / OSINT-ish &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Difficulty:** medium &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Flag format:** `RCC{...}`

## Description

> It's 2010. You finally have a headset, and you want into a real clan.
>
> **[GER] Wasted Gaming | Public TeamSpeak³ Server #1** is still up. The owner, **Marcel**, is online
> most evenings and he is the one who hands out the key to new members. He never writes anything down,
> he does not read chat, and he only talks to people who can prove they belong.
>
> Connect, find him, and get yourself recruited. Everything you need is somewhere on the server.

**Requirements:** Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge with a microphone (speech recognition is done by your
browser). Push-To-Talk is **Ctrl**, see *Options > Capture*.
